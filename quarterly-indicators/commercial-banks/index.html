About this data
Overview of tracked metrics, sources, update frequency & audit disclosures
What financial metrics are tracked on BankStatsNepal?

BankStatsNepal tracks comprehensive monthly and quarterly disclosures for Commercial Banks, Development Banks, and Finance Companies across Nepal:
• Monthly Indicators: Base Rates (%) and Interest Spreads (%).
• Quarterly Financial Indicators: Non-Performing Loans (NPL %), Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR %, Tier 1 %, CET 1 %), CD Ratio (%), Cost of Funds (%), LLP to NPL Coverage (%), Return on Equity (ROE %), and Return on Assets (ROA %).

How is this data collected & verified?

All data is compiled directly from official monthly rate announcements and quarterly financial disclosure statements published by Nepal's Banks and Financial Institutions (BFIs) as mandated by Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) regulations.

How often is the data updated, and what is the audit status?

• Monthly Rates are updated every Nepali calendar month as institutions release disclosures.
• Quarterly Indicators are updated every fiscal quarter (Q1 Ashwin, Q2 Poush, Q3 Chaitra, Q4 Ashadh). Interim reports for Q1, Q2, and Q3 are unaudited, while verified annual financial statements are explicitly marked with an Audited badge.
